# Arithmetic Integration of Newton's Second Law 
Here we apply two methods, Feynman's and Euler's to determine the orbit of a planet that revolves around a star.
The first method includes a Python function to calculate the area of the infinitesimal surfaces produced at each step of the algorithm.
## Purpose
To be used as a basis of simulation, mainly. It can also be used to explore other aspects of orbital mechanics such as
the verification of Kepler's Law, the investigation of different forms of Newton's Law of Gravity and the effect of speed on the orbit
of a planet.
## Assumptions
GM = 1, therefore time is measured in arbitrary *units of time*.
